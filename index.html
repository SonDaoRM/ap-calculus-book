Shared AB core, not a full course

This edition does not include BC-only additions within Units 6–8 or BC Units 9–10. The full AB-versus-BC book scope has not been selected.

These original chapters focus on selected concepts in the eight-unit AP Calculus AB framework, shared with BC. They do not replace a full textbook, all course topics or official exam practice.

Every chapter includes model limits and source links. Diagrams and synthetic data are marked as such. Unit structure follows College Board's AP Calculus course; explanatory references include OpenStax Calculus volumes 1 and 2. This book is not endorsed by College Board.

Unzip the complete folder first. Keep this index beside the chapter HTML files, then open it in Safari. You can also open any chapter on its own. A chat attachment preview may block scripts; use Safari for interaction.
